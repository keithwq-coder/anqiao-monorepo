Zhongke Anqiao (Suzhou) Technology Co., Ltd., located at the Taihu Science & Innovation Center, is a national-level technology innovation enterprise. Leveraging intelligent innovation, it integrates R&D and sales of medical devices, office intelligence, health management, smart medical integration, elderly rehabilitation, mobile applications, and intelligent prevention/control health-monitoring software.
 
Incubated in 2023 at the Suzhou Advanced Technology Research Institute, the company aligns with the national strategy of revitalizing the nation through science and technology, developing intelligent innovative products for various fields. It possesses a core team with years of experience in image-information acquisition, language development, analysis, processing and recognition research, with extensive expertise in intelligent software R&D. Building on the institute's translation of R&D achievements into products and collaboration with 985/211 universities, the company independently developed microfluidic white-blood-cell analyzer software and innovated its contactless health-monitoring product series and multifunctional smart care-bed solution.

A thriving culture makes a thriving enterprise; strong spirit sustains a lasting foundation.
 
Through years of R&D, the company's technical indicators and performance have continuously improved. Centered on artificial intelligence, it provides one-stop services to clients across industries and is committed to building an AI service ecosystem of hardware + software + service + content. With excellent products and quality service, and a management strategy that treats R&D and marketing with equal weight, the company embraces the arrival of the intelligent new era. |
